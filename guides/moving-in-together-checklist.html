Moving In Together Checklist for Couples

Moving in together is easier when logistics and expectations are decided before boxes hit the floor.

Before move-in day

  • Agree on rent, bills, and shared expense model.
  • List what each person brings and what needs to be purchased.
  • Define non-negotiables: sleep schedule, guest policy, work-from-home needs.
  • Set a moving-week timeline with owners for each task.

First week checklist

Home setup

  • Internet, utilities, and address updates complete
  • Essential zones organized (kitchen, laundry, work area)
  • Emergency contacts and documents stored in one place

Relationship setup

  • Define chore ownership for first month
  • Set a weekly planning check-in
  • Schedule one low-stress quality-time block each week

First month review

After 30 days, review what is working and rebalance chores, budget targets, and routine design. Expect iteration.

Treat your first month as a pilot, not a final operating model.

FAQ

What should couples discuss before moving in together?

Discuss money, chores, schedules, boundaries, and how you will handle conflicts and adjustments.

How should couples divide chores after moving in?

Set clear owners per category and review weekly for the first month to rebalance fairly.

When should we create a shared budget?

Before the move. Planning costs early prevents first-month financial friction.
