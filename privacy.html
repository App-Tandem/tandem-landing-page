Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

Tandem ("we," "us," "our") operates the Tandem mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: email and password used to create and access your account.
  • Profile Information: display name, profile photo, theme preferences, currency, week start, and notification/haptics settings.
  • App Content: shared and personal todos, calendar events, and finance entries (amounts, categories, dates, owners).
  • Pairing Data: pairing status and related identifiers to connect accounts.
  • Feedback: issue/feature reports, message details, attachments you choose to upload, app version, and device platform.
  • Analytics Data: usage analytics and device/app metadata collected via PostHog.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the service (authentication, syncing, pairing, content storage).
  • Personalize the app (preferences and settings).
  • Provide support and respond to feedback.
  • Analyze usage and improve the app.

3. Legal Bases (if applicable)

We process data based on:

  • Contract: to provide the service you request.
  • Consent: where you choose to provide optional data or allow analytics.
  • Legitimate Interests: to improve app reliability and performance.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We share data with service providers and processors, including:

  • Supabase (authentication, database, file storage).
  • RevenueCat (subscription management).
  • PostHog (analytics).

We may also disclose information to comply with legal obligations or protect our rights.

5. Data Retention

  • Account and app content: retained until you delete your account.
  • Feedback and attachments: retained as long as needed for support and product improvement, then deleted.

We may retain limited data in backups for a short period after deletion.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may request to access, correct, delete, or export your data. You can also opt out of analytics where available. To exercise rights, contact us at bruno.petkovic.business@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We use appropriate safeguards where required.

9. Children

Tandem is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Cookies & Tracking

The mobile app does not use cookies. We use analytics (PostHog) that may collect device and usage identifiers. You can opt out where available in the app or by contacting us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you in-app or by other reasonable means. The Effective Date above reflects the latest version.
