One shared space for two

Everything you coordinate as a couple, finally together.

Tandem brings the practical parts of shared life and one meaningful weekly ritual into a calm, private app for you and your partner.

Choose the problem you want to solve first

You do not need to move your entire life into Tandem on day one. Start with the recurring coordination problem that creates the most friction, then add the rest when it feels useful.

Shared to-do list for couples Assign clear ownership for groceries, chores, errands and shared plans. Shared couples calendar Keep appointments, date nights and the week ahead visible to both partners. Couple expense tracker Manually log shared spending, splits and categories without connecting a bank. Weekly relationship check-in Reflect privately, reveal together and choose one promise for the week.

Why one app instead of four?

A calendar can show when dinner is. A task app can remember the reservation. An expense tracker can record the bill. Tandem keeps those parts of shared life in one place, so both partners know where to look without coordinating through scattered messages.

Start with one useful shared action.

Invite your partner, add one real task or event, and see whether Tandem makes the coming week easier.
