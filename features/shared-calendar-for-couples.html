A couples calendar should reduce coordination

Most calendar problems are not caused by forgetting to add an event. They happen because one partner does not know the event exists, a work commitment conflicts with a shared plan, or relationship time never becomes concrete enough to protect.

Tandem gives both partners a shared calendar inside the same space they use for tasks, routines and expenses. Each person can still keep personal systems elsewhere; Tandem is where plans that affect the relationship become visible.

Shared by default

Once paired, couple events live in the same shared space. Both partners know where to look without configuring a separate family workspace.

Useful reminders

Set event reminders so plans do not rely on one person remembering for two. Reminder preferences stay practical for the event ahead.

Native calendar context

Bring relevant events from your device calendar into view, then decide what belongs in the shared couple space.

Today on Home

Upcoming shared plans appear with the tasks and priorities that matter today, reducing the need to check several apps every morning.

What belongs in a shared couples calendar?

  • Plans that affect both partners' availability
  • Appointments one partner needs to remember or support
  • Trips, visitors, family commitments and social events
  • Date nights and protected relationship time
  • Deadlines that require shared preparation

Purely personal reminders can stay personal. A useful rule is simple: if the plan changes your partner's day or they would reasonably expect to know about it, put it in the shared calendar.

Set it up without creating another calendar chore

  1. Add the next seven days. Start with real plans, not a perfect historical calendar.
  2. Agree on one rule. Each partner adds plans that affect both of you as soon as they are confirmed.
  3. Protect one piece of relationship time. Put it on the calendar before the week fills up.
  4. Review together once a week. Five minutes is enough to catch conflicts and prepare for busy days.

Shared calendar questions

Does Tandem replace Google Calendar or Apple Calendar?

It does not have to. Keep work and purely personal schedules where they already live, and use Tandem for plans that need shared couple context.

Can an iPhone user share with an Android user?

Yes. Tandem is cross-platform, so both partners use the same shared space even when their phones are different.

Can we schedule date nights?

Yes. Date nights work especially well as shared events because reserving time matters even when you decide the activity later.
