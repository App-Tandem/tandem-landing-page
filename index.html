Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tandem free?

Yes! Tandem is free to download and use. A premium subscription unlocks additional features like unlimited shared lists and advanced budget tracking.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted and stored securely using Supabase. We never sell your personal information. Read our privacy policy for full details.

Is Tandem available on Android?

Not yet — we're actively working on it! Join the waitlist to be the first to know when Tandem launches on Android.

How is Tandem different from Google Calendar or Splitwise?

Tandem combines shared todos, a shared calendar, and shared finances into one app built specifically for couples. Instead of juggling 3+ different apps, everything lives in one place with real-time sync.

Can I use Tandem with a roommate or family member?

Tandem is designed primarily for couples, but it works great for any two people who share a life — roommates, siblings, or close friends who want to stay organized together.
