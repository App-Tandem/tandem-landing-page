Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tandem?

Tandem is a mobile app designed to help couples stay organized together. It lets you manage shared todos, calendar events, finances, and more in one place.

Is Tandem available now?

Tandem is currently in development. Join our waitlist to be notified when we launch.

What can I do with Tandem?

With Tandem, you and your partner can:

  • Manage shared todo lists
  • Coordinate calendar events
  • Track shared finances
  • Stay in sync as a couple

What devices does Tandem support?

Tandem is a mobile app for iOS and Android devices.
