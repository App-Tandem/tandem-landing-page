About Tandem

Built to make shared life feel lighter.

Tandem brings the practical and meaningful parts of a relationship into one calm shared space—from tasks and calendars to money, routines, and a weekly check-in.

Why we built it

Couples often coordinate their lives across messages, notes, calendars, reminders, and finance apps. Tandem is our attempt to reduce that fragmentation without making a relationship feel like a project to manage.

Our product principles

Useful before impressive

Every feature should help two people understand what matters today or take a small shared action.

Private by default

Relationship data is personal. We collect what is needed to operate and improve Tandem, and we do not sell personal information.

Honest about the product

Features, availability, and pricing on this site should reflect the app as it exists today—not a future roadmap.

How we review our guides

Tandem's guides are written to help couples make practical decisions. For product comparisons, we check official product pages and store listings, disclose that Tandem publishes the article, separate verified features from our opinion, and add a review date when the content is time-sensitive.

Guides are not relationship therapy, medical advice, legal advice, or financial advice. If you spot outdated or inaccurate information, email bruno.petkovic.business@gmail.com.
